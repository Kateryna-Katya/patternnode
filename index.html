Velvet Avenue

About the Game

It’s all about your creative vision and whether people actually care about what you’re putting out there
Velvet Avenue focuses on managing a growing fashion empire while balancing design, branding, and public
influence. Players create outfits, choose themes for collections, organize runway presentations, and collaborate
with stylists, photographers, and fashion influencers. We want it to feel glamorous and inspiring, but not too busy
or overwhelming to look at.
